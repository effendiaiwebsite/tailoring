Services

Bring in anything that needs to fit better.

Pricing is quoted up front when you drop the garment off, along with a pickup date. Small jobs are often turned around very quickly.

Alterations & repairs

Any garment, any fabric. Jeans, dresses, suits, jackets, uniforms and everyday clothes.

  • Hemming pants, skirts and sleeves
  • Shortening and slimming shirts
  • Taking in or letting out waists and seams
  • Zipper replacement, patches, torn seams

Custom Afghan & Pakistani clothing

Stitched to your measurements, in the fabric and embroidery you choose.

About the shop

One tailor. Every stitch by hand and by eye.

Needle Art is a small, owner-run tailoring shop on Agnes Street in downtown New Westminster. The tailor trained and worked in Pakistan and Afghanistan before opening here, which is why traditional Afghan and Pakistani garments are a specialty alongside everyday Western alterations.

Customers keep using the same three words: polite, on time, reasonable. That is the whole approach. You get a clear price before any work starts, a realistic date, and a garment that fits.
